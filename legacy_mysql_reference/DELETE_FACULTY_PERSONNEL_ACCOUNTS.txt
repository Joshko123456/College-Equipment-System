ADMIN DELETE UPDATE

People & Accounts now supports Delete for:
- Student Directory (existing behavior)
- Faculty Directory
- Personnel Directory (Staff and other Administrator accounts)

Safety behavior:
- Accounts with no operational history are permanently deleted.
- Accounts referenced by borrowing, approval, damage, assessment, or payment records are deactivated/archived so historical reports remain intact.
- The currently signed-in administrator cannot delete their own account.
- The last active administrator cannot be deleted.
- Archived/deactivated accounts disappear from the active Faculty/Personnel directories and can no longer sign in.
