MOBILE RESPONSIVE UPDATE
========================

The web system now adapts to smartphones and tablets without changing the desktop layout.

Included improvements:
- Dashboard cards reflow for phone widths.
- Wide data tables become stacked, labeled record cards on smartphones.
- Student/Faculty directories remain readable without horizontal overflow.
- Borrowing/return transaction actions fit small screens.
- Equipment cards and images resize to phone width.
- Add/Edit/Create forms become one-column layouts.
- Modals fit within the phone viewport and remain scrollable.
- People & Accounts, notifications, reports, and transaction pages are responsive.
- Touch controls use phone-friendly sizes.
- Student/Faculty welcome and login-related layouts scale down cleanly.
- Landscape phone support was added for short screens.

Desktop and laptop layouts remain unchanged.
