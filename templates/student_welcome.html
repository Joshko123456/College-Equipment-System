BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A little guidance.
A confident start.

How do I get an account?

Students and faculty are first registered by the administrator, then use Activate Account on the login page to create their own private password and accept the borrower agreement. Contact college personnel if your ID is not yet registered.

When can I collect equipment?

Wait for your request to be approved, then coordinate collection with admin or staff. Equipment is assigned to you when personnel release it.

What if an item is damaged?

Report the issue honestly through your borrowing record. Admin or staff assess Fair or Damaged returns; you can review the explanation and cost, then acknowledge responsibility. Good returns have no condition charge.
