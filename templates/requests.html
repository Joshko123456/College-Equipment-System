Equipment Return Process

You can use Return Equipment any time after the equipment is released, including before the due date. Admin and Staff will be notified. Bring the item to them for inspection and return approval. You will receive a notification with the recorded condition: Good, Fair, or Damaged. Fair and Damaged returns require an explanation, responsibility acknowledgment and the assessed condition cost. Good returns include a condition note and PHP 0.00.
