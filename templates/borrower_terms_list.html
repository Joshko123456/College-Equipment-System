Student / Faculty Borrower Agreement

Version 2026-10-04-v1
  1. Account use: Provide accurate information, keep your password private, and use only your own account.
  2. Approved use: Request equipment only for approved academic purposes. Wait for approval and staff release before collecting equipment, and do not transfer borrowed items to another person without permission.
  3. Care and return: Check the issued equipment and serial numbers, report existing defects, handle all items carefully, and return them on or before the recorded due date for inspection.
  4. Report problems: Promptly report any damage or loss. Explain honestly what happened and identify the affected equipment.
  5. Damage responsibility and costs: Fair or Damaged returns may have an assessed repair or replacement cost. You are responsible for damage caused by misuse, negligence, or failure to properly care for borrowed equipment.
  6. Condition assessment: Good returns have no condition charge. Fair or Damaged returns require a condition note, explanation, and responsibility acknowledgment. The exact assessed amount can be reviewed in your borrowing record.
  7. Records and payment: Borrowing requests, returns, explanations, acknowledgments, assessed costs, and payment records are saved by the system. Payments must follow the college-approved process.
